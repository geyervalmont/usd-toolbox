# Third-party fuzz seed attribution

`seeds/gltf/box.seed` is an unchanged copy of the Khronos glTF Sample Assets
Box GLB. Its exact revision, download URL, byte length and SHA-256 are recorded
in [the corpus manifest](../fixtures/corpus/manifest.json).

The upstream model's Legal section reads:

> &copy; 2017, Cesium. [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode)
>
> - Cesium for Everything

Only the filename was changed from `Box.glb` to `box.seed`; the model bytes are
unchanged. The source is the [Khronos Box model](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Box).
